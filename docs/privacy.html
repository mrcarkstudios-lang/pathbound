Privacy Policy for Pathbound

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Developer: Gamics

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/mrcarkstudios-lang/pathbound/issues

Pathbound ("the game") is a free-to-play Android puzzle game. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • Your game progress (cleared levels, hints, settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us.
  • The game has no servers of its own and uses no analytics service of ours.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language),
  • ad and app interaction data, and crash/diagnostic information.

We do not receive or store this data ourselves. Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch one, for example to earn a hint.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) so you can accept or refuse personalised ads. You can reopen it from Settings > Ad privacy choices in the game.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalisation in Settings > Google > Ads (menu names vary by device).
  • You can buy Remove Ads inside the game to stop banner and pop-up ads.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Bundle and hint packs) are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. We only learn from Google Play that a purchase was completed so we can unlock it for you.

4. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Data processed by Google AdMob and Google Play is handled by Google under its own policies.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because progress is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. For data held by Google (for example the advertising ID), use your Google account and Android settings.

7. Security

Ad and purchase traffic is encrypted in transit by Google's SDKs.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and publish the new version at the same web address.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/mrcarkstudios-lang/pathbound/issues
